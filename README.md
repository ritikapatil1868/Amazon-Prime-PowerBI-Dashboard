# Amazon-Prime-PowerBI-Dashboard
# Amazon Prime Streaming Insights Dashboard

## 📌 Short Description & Purpose
This project is an interactive dashboard built in **Power BI Desktop** to analyze Amazon Prime Video’s Movies and TV Shows.  
The goal is to explore content trends, audience preferences, and quality metrics so that streaming platforms can make better decisions.  
It was created as part of my Almabetter coursework to practice data visualization and storytelling.

## 🛠 Tech Stack  
List of key technologies used to build the dashboard:  
- **Power BI Desktop** – Dashboard design and interactive visuals  
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning, editing, and transformation  
- **DAX (Data Analysis Expressions)** – Calculated columns, measures, and advanced calculations  
- **Data Modeling** – Relationships between tables, star schema design  
- **File Format** – CSV dataset provided during training, imported into Power BI  

## 📊 Data Source
Dataset provided by **Almabetter classes** during training.  
It includes metadata of Amazon Prime Video titles such as genre, release year, country, IMDb ratings, and votes.

## ✨ Features & Highlights

### 🔹 Page 1 – Overview
- **Business Problem & Goal:** Streaming platforms need a quick snapshot of their catalog size and growth.  
- **Charts & Insights:**  
  - KPIs: Total Titles (10K), Movies (9K), TV Shows (1K), Avg IMDb Rating (5.98).  
  - Line Chart: Content released over time → shows sharp growth after 2000, peak around 2020.  
  - Donut Chart: Movies vs TV Shows → Movies dominate (86%), Shows only 14%.  
- **Business Impact:** Helps identify imbalance in catalog (too many movies vs fewer shows), guiding future investment in TV content.

---

### 🔹 Page 2 – Content Analysis
- **Business Problem & Goal:** Platforms must know which genres and countries contribute most, and how quality is perceived.  
- **Charts & Insights:**  
  - Bar Chart: Top 5 Genres → Drama leads, followed by Comedy and Documentary.  
  - Gauge Chart: Average IMDb Rating → Moderate at 5.98, shows rate higher than movies.  
  - Bar Chart: Top 5 Countries → US dominates (4810 titles), followed by India and UK.  
- **Business Impact:** Reveals strong genre concentration (Drama/Comedy) and regional dominance (US, India). This helps in planning diverse content and targeting new regions.

---

### 🔹 Page 3 – Audience & Quality
- **Business Problem & Goal:** Platforms need to understand how ratings connect with audience reach and which type of content drives engagement.  
- **Charts & Insights:**  
  - Map Chart: Global content distribution → shows spread across continents.  
  - Scatter Plot: Rating vs Audience Reach → higher IMDb scores correlate with higher votes.  
  - Bar Chart: Content Quality by Type → Shows have higher average ratings than Movies.  
  - Bookmark Toggle: Ratings vs Popularity →
---
- **Business Impact:**  
  - Identify top performing genres and countries for targeted content investment.  
  - Spot trends in release years to plan future production strategies.  
  - Understand audience engagement patterns to improve recommendations and marketing.  

- **Highlights:**  
  - Interactive slicers for filtering by Type, Release Year, and Country.  
  - Clean design with consistent color palette.  
  - Easy navigation across pages: Overview, Content Analysis, Audience & Quality.
